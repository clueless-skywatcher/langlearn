# Esperanto — course outline

Sections follow the order of Ivy Kellerman Reed's *A Complete Grammar of
Esperanto* (1910), whose sixty lessons this course reorganises into CEFR
blocks. **Rule numbers are the book's own**, so ¶16 here is ¶16 there; gaps in
the sequence are the book's exercises, which are not rules.

A **checkpoint** follows every third or fourth lesson section and examines
exactly that block.

Sections marked ✅ are authored.
