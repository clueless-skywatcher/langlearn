# Telugu — course outline

Telugu is written in an **abugida**: a consonant letter carries a vowel inside
it, and marks override that vowel. A learner coming from an alphabet cannot
read a single word until that system is understood, so this course spends its
first five sections and two checkpoints on the script alone — and drills it at
length rather than presenting a chart and moving on.

Rule numbering is continuous. A1 sections 01–03 occupy ¶1–¶19, 04–06 ¶20–¶37,
07–09 ¶38–¶57.

Sections marked ✅ are authored.
